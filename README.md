# The Decision Enabler

**Live:** https://rajneesh05-lab.github.io/decision-enabler/

Three agents help you make a big decision:

- **Agent 1, the Advocate**, builds the strongest honest case for going ahead.
- **Agent 2, the Skeptic**, builds the strongest honest case against it, or for waiting.
- **Agent 3, the Enabler**, applies hard checks (debt-to-income, emergency fund, loan minimums, time frame), weighs both sides and makes the call.

Decisions covered: buying a home, buying a car, choosing a college (or comparing two), moving to a new city, investing money, and any other decision.

For each one it looks up real data: what is near an address, its flood zone and how home values there have moved; 24 investment options with their real history and forecasts; a college's net price, graduation rate and graduates' pay; a car's fuel economy, crash-test stars and recalls; and price levels and taxes in two cities.

Everything runs in your browser. Your answers never leave your device. Picking an address, a city or a car sends only that pick to the public service that looks it up (OpenStreetMap, FEMA or NHTSA); see the privacy policy.

Research figures are dated (checked 18 November 2026) and cited on the page. This is an educational tool, not financial, legal or tax advice.
