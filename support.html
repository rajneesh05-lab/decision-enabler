The Decision Enabler

Help and support

How it works

Pick the decision, fill in your facts, and press Start the debate. Agent 1, the Advocate, argues for it. Agent 2, the Skeptic, argues against it. Agent 3, the Enabler, checks the hard limits (like debt-to-income or an emergency fund) and makes the call: Go ahead, Go with conditions, Not yet, or Don't.

Where do the numbers come from?

Every example value and every fact the agents quote has a source and a date, shown under each box and in "Research behind this call". They are US national figures unless a source says otherwise, so replace them with your own local numbers whenever you can.

Is my data saved?

Only on your device. See the privacy policy.

Is this advice?

No. It is a structured way to think a decision through. Check important decisions with a licensed professional.

Report a problem or ask a question

Open an issue on GitHub. Please do not include personal financial details.
